About

More About Me

My path into UX began with an engineering education, giving me a strong foundation for understanding technical systems and collaborating with the people who build them. Throughout my career, I’ve worked at organizations including Lenovo, Dell Technologies, General Motors, Honeywell, and Northrop Grumman—designing products for environments where usability, accuracy, and trust are essential.

I specialize in simplifying complexity. Whether I’m designing an operational dashboard, an enterprise cloud platform, an administrative console, or an AI-enhanced workflow, I begin by understanding the people, decisions, and systems behind the interface. I use research, usability testing, prototyping, and measurable feedback to turn those insights into practical product improvements.

Today, my work increasingly focuses on AI-native experiences. I explore how prompts, intelligent automation, conversational interactions, and adaptive workflows can enhance human capability without diminishing transparency or control. I believe the best AI experiences should not simply feel innovative—they should be understandable, responsible, and genuinely useful.

I work closely with product managers, architects, engineers, domain experts, and customers throughout the design lifecycle. This collaboration allows me to balance user needs, business objectives, and technical realities while creating solutions that can scale across products and platforms.

Ultimately, I design for clarity: helping people navigate complexity, act on information, and feel confident using the technology in front of them.
