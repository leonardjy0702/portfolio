Iriver Clix

Iriver Clix, US version was a collaboration project between Microsoft, iriver, and MTV Urge music service, to develop a hero product to showcase the launch of MTV Urge. The storefront was also embedded into the Windows Media Player 11. The overall project took about 8 months, working with a developed form factor, modifying the UI to make the user experience more inline with US consumers. The UI was completely redone based on the usability test of the device and its software. Personas were used to evaluate ease of use, playback of music, adding pictures, viewing pictures, creating a playlist, etc. Based on the usability test, we modified the UI significantly to make the MTV Urge/Microsoft/iriver launch successful.
